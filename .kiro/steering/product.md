# Product: GEARBOUND: Wasteland Warriors

An original mobile mechanical strategy and action game. Players wire a 5×5 gear network to power soldiers, turrets and support, then steer the hero Rivet through short three-lane battles.

## Core loop
1. **Workshop (preparation):** buy gears from a seeded shop with mission-scoped battle scrap; place, drag, merge, sell, undo.
2. **Battle:** powered output gears spawn soldiers or fire turret shots on fixed clocks; enemies arrive by authored waves; the hero changes lanes and uses the Wrench Quake special.
3. **Intermission:** a wave reward, fresh offers and a rebuild before the next wave.
4. **Results:** stars, campaign unlocks, daily contracts, the weekly Scrapstorm event, streaks, chests and boosters.

## Rules that never bend
- Original game only. References inform broad mechanics and presentation; never copy their assets, names or levels. `Reference APK/` is local study material and is never committed.
- Offline campaign play never needs a network connection.
- Monetization: optional rewarded ads, interstitials only at natural transitions, fixed-content purchases. No loot boxes, no ads during combat, placement, tutorials or purchases, no ads at startup, no pay-to-win difficulty spikes. The first three missions are ad-free.
- The normal reward is always granted without an ad. Every balance change goes through the wallet ledger with an idempotency key.
- Never mark an unexecuted build, test or device profile as passed.
