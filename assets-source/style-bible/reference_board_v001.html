Tactile Industrial Comic 2.5D: reference board v001

Working board for ART-01. Built only from GEARBOUND's own tokens, materials and layout rules (docs/VISUAL_STYLE_BIBLE.md, MATERIAL_LIBRARY.md). Diagrams, not finished artwork; no reference-game imagery.

Status: concept, awaiting owner review. Nothing here is approved.

Camera: elevated side view, three logical lanes

Lane 0 is far and smallest, lane 2 is near. Scale is visual only; reach and range stay in simulation millimetres. Sort by ground contact, far to near, ties by entity ID.

Palette tokens

Proposed values. Energy Gold, Acid Lime, Enemy Crimson and Special Violet carry gameplay meaning only.

Value bands

Background quiet, gameplay ground quieter in detail, actors carry the full range. Actors need 3:1 contrast against the lane behind them.

Background
mid-light, low saturation, no outlines far away
Gameplay ground
mid value, few details along lanes
Actors and gears
full value range, outlines, highest saturation

Light

Warm key from the upper left, cool sky fill in shadows, contact shadow under everything grounded, energy light only on powered machinery.

Material library

Each swatch shows highlight, base and one cel shadow step from MATERIAL_LIBRARY.md. The painted sheet with worn states is still to be made (style_material_library).

Gears: tiers read by construction, not colour

Beveled teeth with a side face, reinforced rim, spokes and a visible axle. Meshed gears turn opposite ways (rotationParity); unpowered gears stand still.

Shape language and proportions

Silhouettes must read as solid fills at gameplay size. Head heights: hero 4.5 to 5.5, infantry 4 to 4.5, fast enemies lean forward, heavies sit low, the boss is about 2.5 hero heights.

Screen compositions

Shares of the safe area. The board is always 5 by 5; tablets get space, never more capacity.
