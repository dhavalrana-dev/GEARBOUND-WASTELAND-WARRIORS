# UI/UX specification

The board and its powered/unpowered state must remain visible together with combat consequences. Use large touch targets, short labels, high-contrast costs, clear scrap totals, and text/icon redundancy for state.

Portrait: status strip at top, upper three-lane battlefield, hero controls at midline, lower construction board with collapsible shop during battle. Landscape: board and shop in left third, battlefield and controls in right two-thirds. Tablet increases panel spacing and target size rather than increasing gameplay capacity. Respect safe areas and preserve logical state on orientation changes.

Preparation interactions: tap card to buy, drag ghost to a highlighted cell, tap placed gear to rotate/select, merge preview appears on compatible occupied destination, and undo restores the prior board snapshot. Errors explain bounds, occupancy, cost, or connection constraints. Combat HUD shows fortress health, wave, scrap, gear output status, lane buttons, attack, and ability cooldown.
